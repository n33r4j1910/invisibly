🛡️ Invisibly

Before we start protecting this device, a quick, honest summary — then the full documents if you want them.

✅ In plain terms

  • Invisibly does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, telemetry, or usage statistics — everything runs and stays on this device.
  • It monitors local security signals (firewall, updates, network state, running processes) to detect drift and auto-repair what's safe to auto-repair.
  • All data is stored at C:\ProgramData\Invisibly, encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM) and integrity-signed (HMAC-SHA256).
  • System logs are kept for 30 days and auto-rotated. Nothing is shared with third parties or sent outside this device.
  • You can withdraw consent at any time from the dashboard's Disable button, or by uninstalling the app.
