Data Collection and Processing 3.1 Types of Personal Data Collected Invisibly is built to protect your PC without ever learning anything about you. Offline by design: all of Invisibly's protection runs on your own computer. It does not need an internet connection to watch your security settings, spot tampering, repair problems or stop ransomware and hidden crypto-miners. It has no account, no sign-in and no cloud service. The only time Invisibly goes online is an optional once-a-day download of security-list updates (newly found bad programs and websites). This is a one-way download: it fetches a signed list and sends nothing about you or your PC. You can turn it off in Advanced > Protections and everything else keeps working. Updates and Pro subscription status are handled by the Microsoft Store. Two optional online features, and who sees what: (1) the once-a-day security-list download is fetched from GitHub, which can see your internet address, as with any download; (2) Safe Browsing, which is on by default only when your PC takes its DNS from your router or provider (a DNS you or your company set yourself is never overridden), sends the website names you look up to Quad9 (a public DNS service) instead of your usual provider, so Quad9 sees those names. You can switch it off any time in the dashboard. Invisibly sends nothing else to anyone. App updates and Pro subscription status are handled by the Microsoft Store, whose own policy applies. To spot risky browser extensions and hijacked browser settings, Invisibly reads your browser's settings and extension list files (Chrome, Edge, Brave) on your device. It does not read your history, passwords, cookies or page content, and nothing leaves your PC. What Invisibly never captures: your name, email address, phone number, location, passwords, browsing history, documents or the contents of your files. It also collects no device identifiers, advertising IDs, telemetry, crash reports or usage statistics, and it contains no analytics, tracking or advertising code. The security checks it makes (settings, startup items, network configuration, connected devices and similar) are read on your device, judged on your device and stay on your device. 3.2 Processing Activities All operations run locally on the user's device. Data Storage and Security 4.1 Storage Location All data is stored locally on the user's device at: `C:\ProgramData\Invisibly\` 4.2 Security Safeguards - HMAC-SHA256 signatures for tamper detection - The saved baseline and the security timeline are encrypted (AES-256-GCM); the key is protected by Windows (DPAPI) - Windows ACL hardening on data folders 4.3 Data Retention - System Configuration Data: Retained until user uninstalls - System Logs: Kept on your device only; the security timeline keeps the most recent 1,000 events, other logs are rotated by size - Consent Records: Retained as per DPDP Act requirements 4.4 Data Deletion Invisibly keeps its data in C:\ProgramData\Invisibly. Windows does not remove that folder when an app is uninstalled, so to erase everything Invisibly stored, delete that folder after uninstalling. 5. Data Sharing and Transfer 6.1 Third-Party Sharing Invisibly does not share any personal data with third parties. The two optional connections described in section 3.1 reach GitHub (security-list download) and, while Safe Browsing is on (the default on most home PCs; you can switch it off), Quad9. Like any internet service they can see your internet address, and Quad9 sees the website names you look up. Invisibly sends them nothing else. 6.2 Cross-Border Data Transfer Your files, settings and security data are never transferred anywhere and remain on the user's device. The only exceptions are the optional connections described in section 3.1. 6.3 Data Processors The Software does not engage any external Data Processors. 7.
